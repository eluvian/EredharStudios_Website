03 · About

Hi, I'm Andrei.

I'm from Romania. I wrote the fantasy novel Wars of Eredhar out of a love for fantasy and imagined worlds. Since then I've moved on to gaming experiences and, more recently, short films in Unreal Engine 5.

What I enjoy most is learning. Every project makes me pick up a method or a process I didn't know before.

Day to day I use

  • Unreal Engine 5
  • MetaHuman and performance capture
  • DaVinci Resolve
