Dawn of Darkness

The first book of the series Wars of Eredhar, a fantasy novel set in a world full of secrets, mysteries and adventures.

Whispers in the night tell of an ancient darkness re-emerging from the cold corners of Eredhar. The times of peace are fading, and the war to come will test the boundaries of virtue and belief.

The looming threat will not go unanswered, for there will be those who rise up against it. But will they be in time, or will the return of the Darkness be the downfall of every kingdom in Eredhar?
